# Assumptions, limitations, and failure modes

Each item says what could make a conclusion wrong, how the project checks it, and where the evidence is. Notebook 10 writes the same list with file paths to `results/claims/limitations_evidence.csv`.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| L01 | The hedge is a synthetic 10-year constant-maturity par Treasury, reset daily, with excess-return (repo or futures-like) financing | A real desk hedges with Treasury futures or swaps, which add basis, delivery-option, and roll effects, and financing can differ from the bill rate | Financing-spread sensitivity (10 bp a year), 5-year hedge tenor | `results/evaluation/robustness_test.csv` |
| L02 | NMDB quarters are treated as usable one quarter after quarter end | NMDB was first published years after 2013, so the historical availability is approximate | Two-quarter lag rerun | robustness table |

Early test decisions also use proxy-era days inside the hybrid's average share ḡ (legitimately, since the calibration was known by then) | Labeled `proxy_retrospective`. A development-period perturbation test shows the leak explicitly. A robustness rerun removes the proxy entirely (training and ḡ) | `results/forecasts/lookahead_check.json`, robustness table |
| L04 | MBB is measured at market closes | ETF prices can depart from NAV in stress (March 2020) | Rerun excluding March–April 2020 | robustness table |
| L05 | Empirical duration is a regression slope on 10-year yields | It mixes prepayment effects, spread moves that co-move with rates, curve-shape effects, and index composition drift. | L06 | The test period contains only a few refinancing cycles | Wide intervals, low power for gains of a few percent, and results concentrated in a few episodes | Power and false-positive rate from full-pipeline simulation, the interval's upper end against the 5% target, leave-one-episode-out, cumulative-gain plot | `results/simulation/power_summary.json`, `results/evaluation/outcome.json`, `results/evaluation/episodes_test.csv` |
| L07 | Monthly error bars come from about 21 daily observations | Standard errors can be too narrow, which inflates naive tests | HC3 leverage-adjusted Newey-West errors, simulation of coverage, and a constant-duration residual bootstrap for the variation test | notebook 04, `results/evaluation/q1_variation.json` |
| L08 | The aggregate stock note rate (all agency-eligible borrowers) stands in for MBB borrowers' incentive, and dispersion is approximated as normal | MBB's own coupon stack and borrower mix differ, and the real rate distribution can be bimodal (for example many loans near 3% and a newer group near 7%) | All Mortgages market and ±50% dispersion reruns | robustness table, `results/state/state_info.json` |
| L09 | Trading costs are declared assumptions (0, 1, 2, 5, 10 bp per unit of notional traded) | Actual costs depend on instrument, size, and market conditions | Full cost grid and break-even cost per trade | `results/evaluation/cost_grid_verdicts_test.csv`, `verdicts_test.csv` |
| L10 | VMBS tracks a closely related index. PIMCO's GNMA fund differs from MBB in both its mortgage pool (mostly Ginnie Mae) and its active management. | L11 | Freddie Mac changed the PMMS method on 2022-11-17 | A level shift in the incentive could look like a change in refinancing conditions | Not separately tested. A shift of a few basis points is small next to the 2022 move of about three points | notebook 02 figures |
| L12 | The hedge removes only first-order rate risk | Negative convexity and spread risk remain, so even a perfect duration forecast leaves residual risk. ## Failure modes to watch for in the results

* **A gain driven by one episode.** If leaving out one episode removes most of the hybrid's advantage, report the result as environment-limited.
* **A no-gain result whose interval still reaches the 5% target.** Report it as inconclusive, not as evidence of no value. Notebook 04 reports how often a real gain of the simulated size is missed in one test period.
* **Turnover that outruns the risk reduction.** The break-even cost per trade shows how close the result is to that line.
* **Forecast gains without hedging gains.** Lower forecast error need not lower hedged variance, because spread noise dominates weekly P&L. The project reports both.
* **Position-limit hits.** Any hit means a forecast implied a hedge outside 0 to 1.5 times notional. The count is in the main table.
